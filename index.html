About Mineral Exco

Founded in 2021, Mineral Exco (Pty) Ltd is a company specialising in the separation and classification of chromite-bearing material into a concentrated form of chrome ore through the process of crushing, screening and washing. The company has built a reputable name in the industry. It is known for its optimised processes and efficiency in production.
